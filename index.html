What do the ratings mean?+
Season fit runs from 1 (poor fit) to 5 (excellent fit). Projection runs from 1 (skin-close) to 5 (very strong); it is not a distance or a time claim. Originality runs from 1 (familiar) to 5 (experimental), and everyday ease from 1 (challenging) to 5 (easy to place). These are editorial judgments, not quality or attractiveness scores. Night is an occasion, not a fifth season.
Where does the information come from?+
The supplied Fragrance Atlas workbook remains the source for the original 72 profiles. This edition adds 100 editorial profiles; their current bottle prices and source links were intentionally left unverified rather than invented. Original source links were carried over and have not been re-verified for this website. No affiliate tracking has been added. Category names are informal; for example, some designer fragrances appear under everyday value.
Do the avant-garde fragrances contain actual bodily fluids?+
Names such as blood, gasoline, or spinal fluid describe artistic accords and concepts; they are not proof of literal ingredients. Published notes are scent vocabulary, not ingredient lists. Extreme concepts are best explored cautiously as samples, with consideration for other people in shared spaces.
What is stored, and how can I take it with me?+
The shortlist and your notes are saved locally in your browser, not uploaded to a server. Saving depends on browser storage being available; private browsing, file URLs, or clearing browser data may affect persistence. Export your list as CSV to keep a backup. Testing notes are optional. The shortlist does not collect sample costs or calculate a sample budget. All bottle visuals are original illustrations, not the brands' packaging.
